    Bend positions are cumulative distances from the starting solder-pad centre, at the theoretical 90° corners. The cut blank adds 8 mm (4 mm per end) for joints, fitting and small crossing lifts. For preliminary marks measured from the cut end, add 4 mm to each listed bend position. Fit and trim the spare tails; do not treat these as exact finished-wire dimensions. The planar lengths assume 2.54 mm hole pitch; finite bend radius and actual wire height change the finished length slightly.

    01 / FIT

    Place the hardware

    Dry-fit sockets, headers and passives. Trim the antenna notch with the Pico removed. Solder components, leaving the Pico and IC unplugged.

    02 / BRIDGE

    Make the short links

    Follow S01–S12 underneath. Reinforce the long S01 ground bus with a bare tinned wire, soldered to every listed pad. Short links can be solder bridges.

    03 / WIRE

    Lay the insulated jumpers

    Follow W01–W13. Solder stripped tips onto endpoint pads; insulation covers the entire route between them. Lay later wires over earlier ones at crossings.

    04 / CHECK

    Meter before power

    Check ground continuity, 150 Ω from each IC output to J2, and no persistent 5 V/GND short. Verify 5 V at IC pin 14, then power off and insert the modules.

    Wire, solder and the actual board

    Use 26 AWG insulated hookup wire for data and 24 AWG for 5 V / GND. “Insulated” is the important property here; screened/shielded cable is unnecessary for these short on-board links. Trim the cut lengths to fit, strip only about 1–2 mm at each end, and keep insulation intact over every crossing.

    The drawing distinguishes three things: route endpoints are solder joints, smooth wire bends are just bends, and crossings do not connect. A half coordinate such as row 13.5 places a wire between pad rows. It does not mean drilling a hole. Wires attach on the underside of existing solder joints; never force a wire and socket pin into the same hole.

    For a one-gap bridge, use a clipped component lead if solder will not span cleanly. For S01, use about 0.4–0.5 mm diameter bare tinned copper, soldered at every pad in column 12, rows 12–18. Keep the bus flat and separated from neighbouring IC pins. Leave at least 6 mm clearance beneath the finished board for the insulated crossings.

    This is a manually planned routing guide with automated connectivity and bare-link checks, not a fabricated or electrically tested board. Measure actual pad spacing, component bodies and connector footprints. Use it as a build drawing, then verify your soldering with a meter.

    Power, radio and the three outputs

    C2: 220 µF / 16 V electrolytic, ≤8 mm diameter, positive at (5,12), stripe/negative at (5,14). It is useful local supply buffering; 100–470 µF is also suitable if it fits. C1: 100 nF ceramic, ≥16 V, at (15,12) and (15,13), with short local links to the chip’s supply and ground bus. C1 has a prominent yellow label and pointer in the drawing.

    D1 is fitted: 1N5817 Schottky, above the Pico. W03 feeds raw +5 V to its anode at (7,1). Its banded cathode at (3,1) feeds Pico pin 39 (VSYS) through W13. The band is on the left in the assembly view and on the right in the mirrored solder view. HCT pin 14 stays on raw +5 V. Do not fit a jumper around D1. Diode polarity and dimensions.

    The added diode blocks USB power from feeding back into the external 5 V rail. USB and the external supply may be connected together. For USB-only programming, enter BOOTSEL so GP2–GP4 are not driving the unpowered HCT buffer. During normal LED operation, keep J1 powered.

    J2 is A / B / C only, preserving hardware 1.0. Ground remains common via existing power wiring; route each external data wire alongside its own branch ground. The 12 V LED power and strip capacitors stay off this board. Fuse the incoming 5 V upstream.

    The notch removes columns 21–24, rows 5–8, with cuts midway between pads. Keep both header rails intact. Confirm the actual antenna footprint before cutting; the illustrated notch is not an RF clearance certification. No routing enters it. The grid is assumed centered on the 70 × 50 mm blank.
